# Security

If you find a way to identify a contributor from anything this site publishes or holds, a way to
alter or forge the committed log, a way to make the site load anything from another origin, or a
way to make it write something it says it does not write, please tell us before anyone else:
[security@privateanecdata.org]. Plain email is fine; do not include any report content.

In scope: everything in this repository and the deployed site. We publish what we hold and how
we hold it, so most of what a report would normally need is already in `docs/THREAT_MODEL.md`.

What we will do: acknowledge within [three] days, fix, and describe the issue and the fix in the
next release's integrity log (table T14) or, if it affected contributors' expectations, on the
limits page. We do not run a bounty. We will not pursue anyone who reports in good faith and
does not access, retain, or disclose report data beyond what is needed to demonstrate the issue.
